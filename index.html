For Damian ♥

Damian Novick,

I promise this will be my last letter to you, a closure letter for both you and me.

Damian, please know that as I am writing this, I feel very grateful because I had the opportunity to share those moments with you. You are such a meaningful and wonderful person in my life. You are the best boyfriend I’ve ever had, and even the best ex-partner I could have asked for. It hurts me so much to accept that I need to stop being with you. But let’s leave it to God and hope that He will show us a way back to each other if He is willing.

Anyway, I want to tell you that after receiving the message you sent to Z, it made me realize that perhaps it is time for me to walk away and give us both the space we need. That message was very powerful for me and helped me realize what I need to do. I don’t hate you, Damian. I am just very emotional, and I need time to process everything. I hope you understand and can also find peace in moving forward because neither of us deserves to keep getting hurt. I believe this situation has become unhealthy for both of us.

Since you have been honest and transparent with me, I really appreciate that. You did your best, Damian, and I believe you genuinely wanted to work things out. But perhaps it is time for us to move forward separately.

Yesterday, when you told me that you had considered my feelings before Z’s and that you had also hurt her before, I understood more about what you were going through. When you said that you never felt better after making those decisions, it made me think about the time we spent together, too. I sometimes wondered whether you were truly happy with me or whether everything was still weighing on your heart.

It hurts me because I always wished that my presence could bring you happiness and comfort and help you feel better, but I realize that I couldn’t take away everything you were going through. I wanted you to feel better when you were with me. I wanted you to know that you had someone who genuinely cared about you. But I also understand that some feelings cannot simply disappear, no matter how much someone wants them to.

I don’t want to see you continue feeling haunted by what happened or carrying that weight in your heart. It hurts me to think that you might keep struggling with those feelings, even when we are together. I genuinely want you to find peace within yourself, Damian. I don’t want our relationship to become another source of pain or guilt for you.

I don’t blame you, Damian. I know you have a kind heart and that you care about other people’s feelings. At the same time, I have feelings too, and I need to consider my own happiness and peace. I don’t want either of us to keep carrying guilt or feeling like we are hurting someone else. That’s why I believe walking away is the better choice for now.

I hope you continue learning from everything that has happened and treat the people who come into your life with honesty, kindness, and consideration. I genuinely want you to be happy, Damian. I also want to apologize for the times I asked you to block or unfollow Z. I realize that I was acting from my own emotions, and I am sorry if my actions hurt her, too.

I am glad that you have decided to go to Hong Kong and the Philippines. I hope you enjoy your time there, have wonderful experiences, and find the peace you are looking for. Please take care of yourself and be considerate of everyone involved. You deserve to enjoy your time and move forward without carrying unnecessary guilt.

I am truly sorry, Damian. I never intended to hurt you, but I think we need to stop seeing each other for the betterment of both of us. I really appreciate the time you gave me, especially during some of my most difficult moments. Thank you for listening to my stories about Papa and allowing me to share those personal parts of my life with you. That means a lot to me. ❤️

Damian, please remember that I will always love you. You did not deserve to feel that everything was your fault, and I know I also made mistakes through my emotions and immature actions. I am sorry for the times I made things difficult for you.

I am grateful that I learned so much about relationships, especially communication, through our time together. I learned a lot about myself, too. Thank you for being part of my life and for giving me memories that I will always appreciate.

And one last thing: if you are going to the Philippines and continue respecting the physical boundaries we discussed, I truly appreciate that, and I trust you. Please remember that you will always have a special place in my heart.

If our paths ever cross again when the time is right, I would be happy to see you. I don’t want to force anything or make promises about the future. Let’s leave that in God’s hands.

Please keep praying for me, Damian, just as I will keep praying for you. I always ask God to keep you safe wherever you go, to surround you with love, and to let you feel a warm hug through my prayers. I genuinely mean that.

Until next time. ❤️

Written with love, gratitude, and a little hope. ❤️
